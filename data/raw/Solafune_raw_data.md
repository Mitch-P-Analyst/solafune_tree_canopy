- Step 1.
    - View Solafune's data terms and conditions to acknowledge avaiable usecase with the resulting data
        - [View Solafune Rule](https://solafune.com/competitions/26ff758c-7422-4cd1-bfe0-daecfc40db70?menu=about&tab=overview&modal=%22%22)

- Step 2. 
    - Download Solafune raw data
        - [Solafune Tree Canopy Segmentation Data](https://solafune.com/competitions/26ff758c-7422-4cd1-bfe0-daecfc40db70?menu=data&tab=&modal=%22%22)

- Step 3.
    - Organise raw data in project required folders
```
├── data/

│   │ ├── zips/                             # Raw satellite Data ZIP files
```
